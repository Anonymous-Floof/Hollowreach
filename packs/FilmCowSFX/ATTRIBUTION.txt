FilmCow SFX — a sound pack for Hollowreach
==========================================

WHAT THIS IS
  An example resource pack, shipped with the game so there is something real to
  turn on. It replaces 68 of the 78 sounds Hollowreach can play. It is OFF by
  default: enable it in Resource Packs from the main menu or from
  Settings > Audio.

  Sheep, pigs and cows are deliberately absent. The source library has no farm
  animals, and the game's own synthesised bleat, oink and moo are written for
  those creatures specifically. What a pack leaves out keeps the built-in sound,
  and this is that working as intended.


SOURCE AND CREDIT
  Built from the FilmCow Royalty Free Sound Effects Library by Jason Steele
  (FilmCow), from the "Recorded SFX" and "Designed SFX" collections.

      https://filmcow.itch.io/filmcow-sfx

  The clips here are derived from that library: converted to mono Ogg Vorbis,
  trimmed, length-capped and level-matched to the sounds they replace. They are
  not the original files.

  All credit for the recordings themselves belongs to FilmCow. Hollowreach's own
  LICENSE covers the game, not this audio; the library is distributed under
  FilmCow's terms, which are stated at the link above.


HOW IT WAS BUILT
  tools/make_example_pack.py in the Hollowreach repository, which needs a local
  copy of the two FilmCow archives and ffmpeg. It documents every choice: which
  recording stands in for which event, the length cap per kind of sound, and how
  the per-event volumes were measured rather than guessed.


MAKING YOUR OWN
  Press "Create Example Pack" in the Resource Packs screen for an empty pack with
  every event laid out, a README explaining the format, and one folder per sound
  ready to drop a file into.
